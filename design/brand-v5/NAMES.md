# Tuner v5: the name is AIRGLOW

The owner picked **airglow**. Before it sticks, this is a re-check in the Play Store and App Store *as far as web search allows* (I cannot query either store directly). Same ground rules as before: this is a personal app, so a "clash" means confusion with a known app or brand, **not legal clearance**, and nothing here is trademark-cleared.

## Verdict

- **No clash with a streaming, IPTV or TV-player brand.** That is the confusion that matters for this app, and it is clean.
- **A real name overlap in the stores:** searching "AirGlow" on the App Store returns LumenRadio's lighting app first. For a personal, sideloaded or privately installed app that is harmless. If the app is ever published, give it a distinct store title (for example "airglow TV"), and note the DoPchoice trademark application in photo-lighting goods: it is a different class of goods, but get proper advice before publishing.
- **It is better than the alternatives on the list**: airtime (the Airtime group-watch app is the same job), LUMEN, BEAM, AFTERGLOW (all avoided in v4) and FLARE (small echoes: Flare MeTV, FlareFlow).
- **Say it:** AIR-glow, two beats, soft consonants; easy across a room. One caution: it can be misheard as "air glow" (two words), which is harmless.
- **Wordmark:** works as a one-word lowercase logotype (Soft, Outfit Bold, with the descending g) and as a lowercase italic condensed logotype (Sharp, Barlow Condensed ExtraBold Italic).

## Before it sticks

Search "airglow" in the Google Play and App Store apps on the TV and phone yourself (quickest real test), and say it next to "Grizzlies" and the other launcher names.
