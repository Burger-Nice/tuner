Decision: top tabs, not a rail

Why

  • tvOS places the tab bar at the top and orders live apps Live first (Apple HIG: Tab bars; Live-viewing apps). Android TV tabs use a pill indicator for full-page destinations, and say to limit a nav rail to 5 or 6.
  • Width is the scarce resource in a poster grid: a rail takes 80 of 960 px and drops a 6th column; the bar takes 44 px of height, and it scrolls away.
  • One job per axis: left and right move along a row, up and down move between rows, and the bar is one step above everything.

Focus and Back

  • Launch focus: the hero's primary button (Watch live, Resume). One press from open to playing.
  • Up from the first row: top bar, on the current tab. Down: back to the last focused item (remembered per tab).
  • Back from a detail: the grid, same focus. Back from deep in a grid: first press scrolls to the top and focuses the chips; second: top bar; third: Home; then the system.
  • The bar slides away while you scroll down (transform only) and returns on Up or Back. Detail pages hide it.
