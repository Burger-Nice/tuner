Decision: top bar, not a rail

Why

  • Left and Right are the content axis everywhere: rows, the guide's time axis, the grid, the player panels. A rail on the left is the focus-overshoot bug by construction. (UX brief 4.1; Android TV top-nav rule.)
  • tvOS puts tabs at the top and orders live apps Live first (HIG, Tab bars; Live-viewing apps). Search sits first, one Left from Home.
  • The bar slides away (translateY) when focus is below row 1, so it costs no height while browsing.

Focus and Back (final)

  • Entry focus: Home = hero button; Live = the current programme cell; NBA = your team's game; Movies and Series = first card; Search = A; Detail = Play or Resume.
  • Up from the first row of any page = the bar. Fresh press only: an auto-repeated key stops at the edge.
  • Back anywhere in a tab's content = scroll to the top, show the bar, focus the tab. Back on another tab = Home, hero focused. Back on Home = leave. A detail page returns to its card.
  • Game time from deep in a grid: Back, Back, OK = 3 presses.
