airglow sounds

Twelve procedurally generated sounds (node synth.js), 48 kHz, 16-bit, mono. Nothing here is a sample. Audition them against a programme bed, because their whole job is to sit under it.

1. Set up the room

Play the programme bed, set your computer or TV volume so it sounds like ordinary dialogue, then leave it alone. Every sound below is rendered to its real level, so what you hear is what the Philips would play at that volume.

The bed is speech-shaped noise with a syllable rhythm, normalised to -23 LUFS (the EBU R128 programme level) by the same K-weighting the sounds are measured with. Default state: the browser needs one click or key press before it will play any sound.

4. Hold a key

Click the box, then hold ← or → (a TV remote repeats about every 50 ms after a 400 ms delay; a computer keyboard repeats at its own rate). The counter shows how many ticks you hear.

5. A small menu to drive with the keys

Click the stage, then use ←→↑↓, Enter (OK), Esc (Back). Move along the tiles, press OK on a tile (select), go down to Options and press OK (open replaces select), move in the sheet, toggle a row, press Back (close), and bump the edge. Enabling the bump shows what the optional edge sound does.
