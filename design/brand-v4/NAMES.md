# Tuner v4: names

The old name Tuner is retired for this round, **SLATE is off the table** (it is the owner's other project), and the owner wants a name in the spirit of what he likes: on air, light, glow, show time, premiere energy; short, clean, easy to say across a room, a strong one-word wordmark. He also said he likes "airtime" (and TIPOFF's name) a lot.

Same ground rules as `design/brand-v3/NAMES.md`: this is a personal app, so a "clash" means *confusion with a known app or brand*, not legal clearance, and **nothing here is trademark-cleared**. Checks are web searches (App Store and Play listings only as far as the search engine surfaces them), so "nothing found" means "nothing turned up", not "free". Look the final choice up in the stores before it sticks.

## Honest ranking

1. **FLARE** (best sound, best mark, one small clash to check); 2. **AIRGLOW** (the safest of the new names, closest to "airtime", unverified in the stores); 3. **airtime** (his favourite, with a real but mild clash); 4. **TIPOFF** (loved, sports-only); then SKYLIT, PRIMETIME, ONAIR. The four avoids are conflicts, not taste.

## Before any name sticks

Search the exact name in the App Store, Google Play and Google TV's home search; say it next to "Grizzlies", "League Pass" and the other launcher apps; check a short domain only if the app is ever published.
