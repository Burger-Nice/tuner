Tuner v4: three hybrids

His taste signals, absorbed rather than checklisted: airtime's gradient, glow and play button; SLATE's ink, chalk and orchid (the name is retired: it is his other project); TIPOFF's slant as a wipe and layout device, without the lime or the sports-only feel. Red is for LIVE only; there is no amber or yellow; the mark and the lockup never share a screen. All three sit in the same palette so the choice is the idea and the name, not the colour.

Gradient with a purposepink, orchid and deep violet: the gradient is the glow, not a decoration. Nearest brand colours: Twitch (deep violet, 12.5), YouTube (pink, 18.9), see each card.
Slant as motionthe wedge and the wipe are the transition language, not just the look: every launch animation ends in a slanted reveal.
