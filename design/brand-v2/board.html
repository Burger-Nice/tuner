Tuner · brand v2 · "Needle"

Locked on.

Tuner is one person's private broadcast desk: every channel, game and film you pay for, with nothing in the way. The mark is a dial scale with the needle locked through it — the moment static snaps into picture.

Mark anatomy · 100 grid

Scale bar 88×18, nine ticks 3×9 knocked out every 8 units. Needle 14 wide at the scale, 20 at the base (the taper is the only soft move). Needle base = wordmark stroke. Dotted circle = Android adaptive safe zone when used as an icon.

Minimum sizes

Mark: 24px min (ticks drop below 32px → mark-small.svg). Lockup: 90px wide min on desktop, 140px on TV. Clear space = half the mark's height on every side; nothing inside it, not even the live dot.

Android TV / Google TV banner · 320×180

Full lockup (icon + text, as Google requires), inside the central 80×60% safe area. Flat #0D0E12, no border, no glow, no gradient: it stays crisp next to the launcher's blue tiles and survives the row's scaling. Ship 320×180, 480×270, 640×360.

One brand colour. Amber is luminous (L ≈ 0.53) so it reads on luminance alone across the room and after 4:2:0 chroma blur, and it does not bloom the way saturated red or blue does on an LCD panel. Red is reserved for LIVE. Nothing darker than #13 is used for a surface, so no step disappears into TV black crush.

Do

  • Mark on bg-0/bg-1 only. Wordmark in text-1, mark scale in accent, needle in text-1.
  • Amber for the mark, one primary action per screen, the selected tab, progress, "your team".
  • White 3px focus ring + lift for d-pad focus. Red dot + "LIVE" text together, never the dot alone.
  • Archivo wide for titles and scores; Inter 500+ for everything else; nothing under 13px on TV.
  • Flat colour. Elevation by surface step, not shadow. Lines ≥ 2px on TV.

Don't

  • No gradients, glows, drop shadows or radial "tally" halos on the mark or the UI chrome.
  • No red buttons, red headings or red brand use. Red means on air.
  • No scanlines, waves, antenna arcs, ribbons or second colours added to the mark.
  • No rotating, outlining or recolouring the needle; no tiled or patterned marks.
  • No Bricolage, Figtree, JetBrains Mono or system fonts in product UI.
