Tuner is one person's private broadcast desk. The T is the logo: its stem is the needle and it cuts the scale in two. T+UNER, one word, one amber letter, no second T.

Mark anatomy · the T grid (cap height 100)

Crossbar 84×23 (thickness = wordmark stem). Stem 23, straight, full height. A 3-unit channel on each side of the stem makes the needle a separate shape, so the idea survives greyscale and blur. Dotted circle = the Android adaptive safe zone; the bar's corners sit inside it.

v2.1 → v2.2: 97-wide bar → 84; tapered 22→28 stem → straight 23; six ticks → none (they read as teeth); white needle → amber channel. Stem-to-U 44.5 → 38.5.

Minimum sizes

Full mark from 64 device px tall (the channel is 3% of height: 1.9 px there). Below that use mark-small.svg; nothing under 16 px. On a 2x screen that is 32 CSS px. Lockup 90 px wide desktop, 140 px on the TV canvas. Clear space = half the cap height.

Rail + splash

Rail: the amber T alone at 44 px CSS (88 device px on the TV). Boot splash and sign-in: the lockup at 200 px CSS. In the lockup the mark is the T at 1:1; never a second mark next to it.

Launcher: amber banner + amber icon

Banner 320×180: ink lockup on amber (10.5:1), cap height 46, in the 80×60% safe area. In a row of dark and blue tiles a dark banner disappears (checks/study22/banners.png); the dark version (left) is kept as the quieter alternative. The icon is an amber tile with an ink T for the same reason.

Rules

  • Do the lockup on bg-0/bg-1 (amber T, text-1 letters) or all-ink on amber (icon, banner) or all-ink on light.
  • Do amber for exactly four things: the mark, one primary action per screen, the current selection, progress. "Your team" uses the team's own colour.
  • Do focus = white 3px ring with a 2px canvas gap + lift. State = icon + word + colour, never colour alone.
  • Don't set a second T next to the mark, or the word without its mark-T ("UNER" is not a wordmark by itself).
  • Don't gradients, glows, shadows, scanlines, arcs, ribbons or a third colour on the mark.
  • Don't use amber for warnings, red for buttons or brand, a status colour as a fill behind text, or pure #000 / #FFF surfaces.
  • Don't add ticks, a pointer above the bar (reads as a cross), a second colour or a taper to the T; keep the 3-unit channel (full mark) and drop it only in mark-small.
